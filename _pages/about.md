I am a Master's student in Electrical Engineering at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/). In Summer 2026, I worked as a Research Assistant at the [ETAIC (Embodied Technology for Autonomy, Intelligence, and Control) Lab](https://etaic.github.io/), The University of Texas at Arlington, advised by [Prof. H. Eric Tseng](https://www.nae.edu/248787/Dr-Hongtei-E-Tseng), a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://www.nae.edu/), and [Dr. Hao E. Zhang](https://haozhang-thu.github.io/), in collaboration with [Prof. Ding Zhao](https://www.meche.engineering.cmu.edu/directory/bios/zhao-ding.html)'s [Safe AI Lab](https://safeai-lab.github.io/) at Carnegie Mellon University.

My research focuses on robot learning for human–robot collaboration, including multi-agent reinforcement learning, safe control, whole-body control of humanoid and quadruped robots, and vision-language models (VLMs) for embodied decision-making. I am a co-first author of a paper published in iScience and have co-authored four papers submitted to ICRA 2027. My work has been deployed on Unitree G1 humanoids and Go2 quadrupeds, including human–humanoid collaborative transport and robot soccer penalty kicks.

# 💻 Research Interests

- Multi-Agent Learning & Game Theory: multi-agent reinforcement learning for human–robot collaboration, self-play, and strategic decision-making.
- Safe Robot Learning: control barrier functions, safety-aligned policy optimization, and interpretable policies.
- Humanoid & Quadruped Control: whole-body control, locomotion, and sim-to-real transfer.

[Anticipatory Robot Goalkeeping via Monotone Optimal Stopping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23976)

Hao E. Zhang\*, Ruize Geng\*, **Yisen Li**, Yaru Niu, Yikai Wang, Raihan Haque, Khalil Zbiss, Guanyang Luo, Hui-ping Wang, H. Eric Tseng, Ding Zhao
<br>*Submitted to IEEE Trans. (Full version) and ICRA (Conf version)*

- We formulate anticipatory robotic interception as a finite-horizon optimal stopping problem and propose monotone optimal stopping (MOS), allowing a quadruped goalkeeper to decide when to act before shot intent is fully resolved while retaining closed-loop adaptation to deceptive direction changes.

[Learning-Augmented Optimization and Control of Long-Haul Mobility Propulsion Systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.115579)

**Yisen Li**\*, Mubai Ding\*, Chaoyi Chen, Nuo Lei, Hao Zhang
<br>*iScience, 29(5): 115579, 2026*

- We propose a hierarchical learning-augmented co-design framework for ammonia–hydrogen hybrid electric heavy-duty vehicles: RL-ANSGA (a TD3 meta-controller that adapts NSGA operators) sizes the powertrain, while a DQN learns real-time energy management and on-board hydrogen production. The optimized vehicle outperforms diesel baselines in energy efficiency and well-to-wheel CO₂ emissions.
